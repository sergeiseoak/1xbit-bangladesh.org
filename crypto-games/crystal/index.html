Crystal at 1xBit – Crypto Slot Rules and Gemstone Combinations

Crystal is a gemstone-matching casino game from the 1xGames collection. Instead of spinning traditional reels, it uses a grid filled with coloured crystals. Winning groups disappear, new gems fall into the empty spaces, and the process can continue as long as fresh matches form.

The game's central rule is simple: five or more connected crystals of the same colour form a winning cluster. Crystals must touch horizontally or vertically; identical gems scattered around the board do not count as one combination.

Unlike a conventional five-reel slot, Crystal does not rely on fixed paylines. Winning combinations are determined by connected groups on the board.

How to Play Crystal

The game begins when the player selects a stake and starts a round. Coloured gemstones fall into the grid, where the system checks for connected matching groups.

The basic sequence is:

Open Crystal in the available game catalogue.

Choose the stake for the round.

Start the game and wait for the grid to fill.

Look for groups of at least five connected crystals of one colour.

Winning crystals disappear and are replaced by new ones.

The sequence continues until no further winning clusters form.

The cascade feature is what gives Crystal its distinctive rhythm. A single starting board may generate several consecutive matches without a new manual selection.

How Crystal Cascades Work

Suppose five red crystals form a connected group. Once that combination is registered, those crystals disappear.

New gems then fall into the empty spaces. If the replacement gems create another valid group, the game processes that match as well.

A cascade can therefore extend a round beyond its initial result. However, additional matches are not guaranteed. The sequence ends when the board no longer contains a qualifying combination.

How Crystal Payouts Work

Crystal uses the number of connected matching gems and the value assigned to their colour to determine a result.

The official 1xBet rules document describes a two-round, two-sided format in which points are calculated using the number of elements in a combination multiplied by the applicable odds. The side with the higher total wins, with a draw possible if the scores are equal.

Other regional 1xBet pages describe Crystal as a casino slot that awards payouts directly for matching clusters.

These are materially different descriptions of the settlement process. Without checking the specific game build offered by 1xBit, it would be misleading to present either payout model as definitively applicable there.

Why the Paytable Matters

Two combinations containing the same number of crystals may have different values if their colours carry different odds.

Likewise, a larger group can have a different reward from a group containing only five gems.

Before placing a real-money bet, check the live game's information panel for the colour values, payout calculation and whether the result is settled as a direct slot payout or a two-sided contest.
