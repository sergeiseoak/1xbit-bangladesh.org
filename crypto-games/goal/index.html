What Counts as a Goal?

A Goal selection wins when the game's final result shows a successful shot.

The animation may depict a striker shooting past the goalkeeper, but the essential condition is the registered game result.

What Counts as No Goal?

No Goal covers an unsuccessful attempt. Published descriptions include a goalkeeper save or a shot that misses the target.

A missed shot and a saved shot are not separate betting markets in the documented two-option version.

What Is the RTP of Goal?

Some regional 1xBet pages publish RTP estimates for Goal!, but those figures are not consistent enough to establish a verified return for the current 1xBit version.

A multiplier of x2 does not prove a 100% RTP.

For example, if the actual chance of a correct prediction were 49%, a x2 payout would produce:

Theoretical RTP = 0.49 × 2 × 100 = 98%

The corresponding theoretical house edge would be 2%.

This is a mathematical illustration, not a confirmed probability or RTP for Goal! at 1xBit.

To calculate the actual theoretical return, both the winning probability and payout multiplier must be known.

Can You Predict Whether the Shot Will Score?

Goal! is not a skill-based shooting game. The player chooses a predicted outcome rather than controlling the shot.

Watching previous rounds doesn't provide a reliable way to determine what will happen next if the results are generated independently.

For example, several consecutive goals do not automatically make No Goal more likely in the next round.

The main decisions under the player's control are the selection and stake amount. Neither guarantees a winning result.
