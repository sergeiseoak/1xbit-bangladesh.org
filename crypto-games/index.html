Instant and Crash Games

Instant games typically use short rounds with straightforward decisions. Rather than waiting through a conventional slot sequence, the player chooses an amount, makes a selection or watches a multiplier develop.

Popular formats include Dice, Plinko, Mines and crash games.

In a crash game, the multiplier increases until the round ends. Players who choose to cash out before that point receive a payout based on the multiplier reached. Dice games instead settle according to a specified number or range.

What to Check Before Choosing a Game

A game title alone doesn't tell you much about how it plays. The most useful details depend on the format.

For a slot, look at the reel layout, paylines or ways to win, bonus features and published RTP. For poker or another card game, the important information is the hand ranking, betting sequence and payout table.

Crash and dice games have different considerations: multiplier behaviour, settlement rules and whether the game provides a way to verify individual outcomes.

Where the developer publishes an RTP, it describes a theoretical long-term return across many rounds. It doesn't predict what will happen in a particular session.

Our game guides aim to identify those details for each title without applying generic slot descriptions to unrelated games.
